The Unbroken Sky: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

The Unbroken Sky is a single-player story game made by Cacious Siamunyanga and published by Mulawo. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it.

What we collect

Nothing. The game does not collect, store or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not connect to the internet while you play.

What stays on your device

The game saves your story progress and your settings (text size, text speed, sound, flight assist, shake and vibration) in a small file on your own device so you can continue later. This file never leaves your device. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Permissions

On Android the game asks only for permission to vibrate the phone, used for feedback during combat. You can turn vibration off in the pause menu.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 17 and over. It collects no information from anyone, including children.

Stores and websites

If you download the game from Google Play or play it on itch.io, those services have their own privacy policies, which apply to your use of their stores and websites.

Changes

If a future version ever collects any information, for example optional feedback, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described here.

Contact
