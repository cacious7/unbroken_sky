# Futuristic civilisation, first-person play

28 October 2026. This is the current owner-directed visual and control baseline. It supersedes rural/precolonial scene dressing and overhead gameplay in earlier notes.

The kingdom achieved substantial engineering, medicine, fabrication and public infrastructure before its fall. A poorer counterpart inherits that knowledge and technology. Its disorder is crowded additions, mismatched repairs, missing components, damaged services and unequal maintenance. Houses and workshops should communicate developed urban life. Regional landscape, material craft and ecological identity remain meaningful; they do not set a ceiling on technological development.

## Camera and action

Normal on-foot play is at Tavo's eye height. Mouse or right-thumb gestures look; keys or the left stick move relative to that direction. Left click/E or the contextual action button uses an object in front. There is no gameplay tap-to-walk. A small reticle, direction arrow and distance provide guidance without exposition. Hands, a held repair hammer, pickup/reach motion and the collected pin place interaction in the player's view. The body renders as a shadow rather than inside the camera.

Dialogue, choice and pause block movement/look appropriately. Repairs stay in the first-person position. Brief authored closeups and fall/transformation shots may temporarily direct the camera. Eagle control is forward-facing first person, with peripheral wings and existing wind/landing mechanics.

The prototype now uses terraced multi-storey housing, rooftop service equipment and planters, external service trunks, utility carriers, a district relay, a manufactured water intake/manual bypass station, parts caddies and paved courts. The flooded capital gains high-rise civic structures and broken transit links. Shared materials and connectors tie the poorer district to the fallen capital. Static architecture is batched by material to limit draw calls.

## Play remains central

These spaces support direct movement, physical repair, readable threats, optional wind gates and quick retries. Poverty is not another technology level, and futurism is not a layer of neon applied to the same huts. Environmental detail should show what the civilisation built, how people use it, and which parts have stopped working.

## Current production limits

The characters and first-person hands are still prototype assets. Reach/swing gestures are not full hand-contact IK, and the form change is staged rather than a continuous anatomical morph. The collision/movement model retains its 2D footprint with explicit 3D stair heights; this is not a full jumping/climbing physics controller. Recorded performances, finished character art and device profiling remain production work. Desktop functional and visual checks do not establish photorealism, fun for uncoached players, or phone frame rate.


## Physical feedback pass

Repair taps now animate hands and handle on both successes and misses. The manufactured outlet has a gravity-fed water stream; fitting the pin stops the leak and restores the mint service indicator. Subsequent pulls briefly release clear water, then contaminated water. Combat uses short hit confirmation marks and a fading arc towards the accepted damage source; grace-period hits do not replace it. The obsolete overhead building-fade pass and recursive floating-label scans were removed.
