# Character voice clips

No character performances are shipped yet. Place licensed or recorded performances here as LINE_ID.ogg (preferred) or LINE_ID.wav; DialogueBox finds them automatically. A line can instead declare a res:// voice path. Speech is never synthesized by the game and never needs a network connection.

Ambient and cinematic lines advance when the clip ends. Without a clip, they use a short reading-time fallback. Choices and posted text remain manual. Keep subtitles short and retain the speaker name. Record conversational intention, breaths, interruptions and local pronunciation with performers; do not imitate real people without consent.

Use docs/GAMEPLAY_DIRECTION.md for delivery and staging. Audition the opening before commissioning the whole chapter. Sound settings mute voices as well as effects. Test silent play and missing clips.
