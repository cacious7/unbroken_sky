# Decisions and revision log

Version 1.1, 27 October 2026. Owner: Cacious Siamunyanga. Development responsibility: owner building the game, with assistance. No team members, partners or paid contributors are assumed.

## Preserved from the brief

Zambia first, with the possibility of reaching wider African audiences. Minimal cash budget and solo development. Mobile as the first target. Story led fantasy, memorable characters, witchcraft and magic, shapeshifting, politics, spiritual traditions, horror and a fallen civilisation. A dry upland homeland inspired partly by Maamba and a richer river world. Eagle as the central animal form, expressing freedom, resilience and power. A protagonist with deeply personal stakes and unknown secrets. A great civilisation with real achievements and concealed faults. A story intended to encourage empathy, responsibility and appreciation of difference. Engagement through enjoyment rather than punitive retention mechanics.

## Settled by this draft

Working title The Unbroken Sky. Tavo is male, age 24, an ordinary courier and repair assistant. His native form is eagle; Kesi lends the hyena form. His secret concerns childhood captivity and Mera's history, not a hidden royal bloodline. The letter supplies the most personal reveal. The catastrophe has multiple concrete causes, with differentiated responsibility. No Christianity appears in this invented setting. Religious belief has constructive and coercive expressions. There is no sexual content or romance subplot. Cultural difference is not an enemy category.

The compact complete game has three acts, 26 numbered scenes, with SC25 split into two authored ending sequences. Four major choice points use reconverging branches. Visible preparations unlock shared containment. The shared ending is the intended basis for any later story, but the central ending is complete. The prologue reappears with another viewpoint rather than being declared false.

## Earlier ideas superseded

The earlier lost-city memory erasure premise and water-failure opening are not combined into two unrelated mysteries: the current water threat belongs to the covenant's breakdown. No unrelated missing-child quest is adopted from the literary reference. The hero is not personally guilty for having been rescued at seven. His birth mother's fate remains unknown; this is a personal future possibility, not an unresolved requirement for understanding the catastrophe. Shapeshifting is not a magical lie detector. We do not use an ancient Christian church, modern flag, real sacred mask enemy or colonial-era ceremony as an allegedly timeless authentic detail.

The mother's first proposed accidental secrecy is sharpened into genuine prior complicity. Daru's exclusive bargain mirrors it without equating the degree of harm. A simple self-sacrifice ending is retained as a troubling possible outcome, not the sole heroic answer. The practical alternative must be built before the final choice.

## Deliberately open

Final names and pronunciation after local review. Final art style after the first greybox. Exact spoken language mix and translation budget. Final title after clearance. Engine version after hardware check. Device performance and download targets after measurement. Chapter duration, commercial price and release date after a playable slice. No claim is made that desk research can certify authenticity or predict emotional impact.

## Revision protocol

When altering a reveal, check its earlier clues and later dialogue. When changing the ending, update the state specification and its repair paths. Do not add a new form, map or major character without naming the production cost. Preserve versioned copies when beginning substantial revisions. For public credits, list people only after actual contribution and consent.
